# Territory Allocation Policy

## Purpose

The territory allocation policy defines how enterprise accounts should be assigned to sales territories.

## Primary Allocation Rule

Enterprise accounts should primarily be assigned based on the geographic location of the customer's major office or operating region.

## Geographic Rules

- Hyderabad accounts should generally be assigned to the Hyderabad territory.
- Bangalore accounts should generally be assigned to the Bangalore territory.
- Chennai accounts should generally be assigned to the Chennai territory.
- Mumbai accounts should generally be assigned to the Mumbai territory.
- Delhi NCR accounts should generally be assigned to the Delhi NCR territory.

## Enterprise Account Priority

Enterprise accounts receive higher priority for territory planning because they generally represent larger and more strategic opportunities.

## Unassigned Enterprise Accounts

When an enterprise account is unassigned, the recommended territory should first be determined from its primary operating location.

If geographic information is ambiguous, the account should be flagged for manual review rather than making an unsupported assumption.

## Recommendation Guidelines

Territory recommendations should consider:

1. Geographic alignment
2. Account segment
3. Existing territory coverage
4. Strategic account importance
5. Sales representative capacity

Recommendations should be presented as recommendations, not as confirmed assignments.

## Important Rule

The system must not claim that an account has been officially assigned unless the assignment exists in the database.
