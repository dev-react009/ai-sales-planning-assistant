# Account Management Policy

## Purpose

The account management policy defines how customer accounts should be managed.

## Account Segments

Accounts are categorized into:

- Enterprise
- Mid-Market
- SMB

## Enterprise Accounts

Enterprise accounts should receive higher planning priority because of their strategic importance and potential business impact.

## Account Assignment

Every actively managed enterprise account should have an appropriate territory and sales representative assignment.

## Unassigned Accounts

An account is considered unassigned when it does not have a sales representative assigned in the system.

Unassigned enterprise accounts should be reviewed as a priority.

## Territory Recommendations

For an unassigned enterprise account, territory recommendations should consider:

- Geographic location
- Industry
- Account segment
- Existing territory coverage
- Sales representative capacity

The system should not modify account assignments automatically.

Recommendations require human approval before an assignment is changed.
