# Sales Quota Policy

## Purpose

The quota policy defines how sales representative performance is evaluated.

## Quota Attainment

Quota attainment is calculated as:

Achievement / Quota × 100

For example, if a representative has a quota of 10,000,000 and achieves 6,000,000:

60% = 6,000,000 / 10,000,000 × 100

## Performance Threshold

Sales representatives below 70% quota attainment should be considered below the expected performance threshold.

## Performance Categories

- Below 70%: Requires attention
- 70% to 89.99%: Developing toward target
- 90% to 99.99%: Near target
- 100% or above: Meets or exceeds target

## AI Response Rules

When asked about quota performance, the system should calculate attainment from the database rather than relying on memorized values.

The system should clearly distinguish between calculated performance and recommendations.

The system must not invent quota or achievement values.
