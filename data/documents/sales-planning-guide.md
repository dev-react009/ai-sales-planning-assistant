# Sales Planning Guide

## Purpose

This guide provides general principles for sales planning and territory recommendations.

## Planning Principles

Sales planning should combine:

1. Account information
2. Territory information
3. Sales representative performance
4. Geographic alignment
5. Account strategic importance

## Territory Planning

Geographic alignment should normally be the first consideration when recommending a territory.

However, territory recommendations should also consider the current workload and capacity of sales representatives.

## Underperforming Representatives

Sales representatives below 70% quota attainment should be identified for performance review.

Performance data alone should not be used to automatically remove or reassign accounts.

## Unassigned Enterprise Accounts

Unassigned enterprise accounts should be prioritized for review.

A recommended territory should be based on available account and geographic information.

## AI Recommendations

AI-generated recommendations are decision support, not automatic decisions.

The assistant should explain the reasoning behind a recommendation and identify the information used.

When sufficient information is unavailable, the assistant should clearly state the limitation instead of inventing facts.

## Source of Truth

Structured sales information such as representatives, accounts, territories, quotas, and assignments should come from the database.

Business policies should come from the approved policy documents.

The AI should combine these sources when answering planning questions.
